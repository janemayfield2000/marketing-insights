<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #ffffff; margin-bottom: 16px;">What to Actually Tell Visitors on Your Coming Soon Page (and What to Leave Out)</h1>
<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/c804394d19a558368340f5d774796e0e.jpg" alt="What to Actually Tell Visitors on Your Coming Soon Page (and What to Leave Out)" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 20px 0;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">A coming soon page needs to answer essential product questions without turning into a full spec sheet. Visitors need just enough detail to decide whether they're a fit: the problem you're solving, who it's for, the expected outcome, and how much of that exists today.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Start with the problem, and make it specific enough to be instantly recognizable. A broad problem statement leaves visitors unable to judge whether your offer applies to their exact situation — and uncertainty kills signups faster than an honest limitation ever would.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Describe your first version honestly, including what it does not yet include. Founders often hide limitations to protect excitement, but transparent scope actually improves subscriber quality and reduces support friction once the product ships. Add a short "who this is for" section too — mentioning role, stage, or use case makes fit obvious at a glance.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Timeline language should stay realistic rather than locked to an exact date: something like "first cohort planned for early Q3, staged rollout based on onboarding capacity" communicates direction without overpromising. A small status marker such as "design complete, onboarding in testing" adds concrete, trust-building context when updated consistently.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">This full approach to product transparency and audience framing is explored further in this <a href="https://unicornplatform.com/blog/coming-soon-page-success-6-best-practices-you-need-to-know/" style="color: #4da6ff; text-decoration: underline;">guide to writing effective pre-launch pages</a>, which also covers how to keep messaging consistent through launch.</p>
