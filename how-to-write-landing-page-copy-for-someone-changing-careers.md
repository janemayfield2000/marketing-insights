<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #ffffff; margin-bottom: 16px;">How to Write Landing Page Copy for Someone Changing Careers</h1>
<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/461250dd63e61d895afa13c1997dca0f.jpg" alt="How to Write Landing Page Copy for Someone Changing Careers" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 20px 0;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Switching careers is rarely just a logistics problem — it's an emotional one. Someone considering a career-transition program is usually carrying real anxiety about whether it's too late, whether they're qualified enough, and whether the financial and time investment will actually pay off. A landing page that ignores this and just lists course topics is missing the actual decision being made.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">The stronger version of this page opens with a persona-specific statement that names the exact transition being made — from teaching into tech, from retail into design, whatever it is — rather than a generic "change your career" headline that could apply to anyone.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">From there, a transition roadmap with visible milestones does a lot of the trust-building work. It shows the path isn't abstract; there's a sequence, a shape, an end point. Pairing that with role-alignment examples — real job titles or outcomes graduates moved into — makes the promise feel concrete instead of aspirational.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">Career support and advisory services deserve real visibility here too, not a buried FAQ link. People switching careers often need reassurance that they won't be doing this alone, and a page that shows ongoing support tends to convert better than one that only sells the curriculum.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">This archetype, along with practical examples of what to include, is broken down well in this <a href="https://unicornplatform.com/blog/7-stunning-education-landing-page-examples/" style="color: #4da6ff; text-decoration: underline;">education landing page framework</a>.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 16px;">The programs that get this right aren't selling a curriculum. They're selling permission to make the leap.</p>
