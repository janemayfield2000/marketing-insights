<h1>The Bootstrapped Founder's Case for Outsourcing Directory Submissions</h1>

<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/759ae5d2867a50643baeca3d973a348e.png" alt="Directory submission">

<p>Bootstrapped founders tend to default to doing everything themselves, and directory submission often lands on that pile — a task that feels simple enough to handle personally, one evening at a time. In practice, it's one of the worst uses of a founder's limited hours.</p>

<p>The actual time cost is hidden in the details: reformatting descriptions to fit different character limits, picking the right category on unfamiliar platforms, tracking which sites accepted the listing and which are still pending. None of this is hard, but all of it adds up to hours that could go toward product or customer conversations instead.</p>

<p>There's also an opportunity cost most founders don't account for. A hundred-plus directory submissions done properly, with correct categorization and paced publishing, produces real discovery and authority value — but only if it's done right, and doing it right manually is exactly what eats the time in the first place.</p>

<p>Outsourcing this specific task doesn't require giving up control. A review-and-approve step before publishing means the founder still decides what represents the brand, without personally filling out a hundred forms to get there.</p>
