<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #111; margin-bottom: 16px;">How Long Does a GPU Actually Last? The 3-5 Year Rule Explained</h1>

<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/31d66d8f433aeeb19730f099bdd25bbe.png" alt="GPU Lifespan" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Most people only start asking about GPU lifespan once a card feels slower, louder, or less predictable than it used to. But age alone rarely tells the full story. The real question is whether your graphics card still delivers the output you need at a cost you're willing to pay — in electricity, in noise, and in troubleshooting time.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">A well-built GPU can technically keep running for many years past its prime. The trouble is that "still running" and "still optimal" are two very different things. For most setups, a 3-5 year window remains the most realistic planning horizon, though workload intensity, cooling quality, and duty cycle all push that number up or down.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">What's changed recently isn't just raw hardware speed — it's how people use their cards. Long AI inference sessions, heavier creative export pipelines, and stricter uptime expectations have all reshaped what "wearing out" looks like. A card that passes a quick benchmark can still buckle under a sustained, real-world workload.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Instead of tracking one vague lifespan number, it helps to separate distinct signals: whether the card still functions, whether it still hits your performance targets, whether temperatures are creeping upward, and how much manual babysitting it now requires. When several of these start moving in the wrong direction at once, that's your actual signal to plan a change.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">For anyone who wants a structured way to track this over time rather than guessing, this <a href="https://unicornplatform.com/blog/how-long-should-a-gpu-actually-last-expect-3-5-years/" style="color: #4f46e5; text-decoration: none; font-weight: 600;">detailed GPU lifecycle breakdown</a> lays out a monthly and quarterly review system that keeps replacement decisions evidence-based instead of reactive.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Treat your GPU's lifespan as a moving target shaped by outcomes, not a fixed expiration date carved into the box it came in.</p>
