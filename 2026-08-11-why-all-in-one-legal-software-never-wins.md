**Why \"All-in-One\" Legal Software Never Wins the Listing Game**

![](https://jpcdn.it/img/small/1825a784271b3d3c879f499db2e5eba9.png)

Legal-tech vendors love the phrase "all-in-one platform for modern law firms." It sounds impressive on a homepage and useless on a software directory. Buyers scanning G2 or Capterra aren't browsing for vibes — they're filtering by category, workflow, and use case. A vague pitch gets filtered out before a human even reads it.

**Specificity Beats Adjectives**

The fix isn't more adjectives. It's specificity. "Contract review and negotiation workflow software for in-house legal teams managing high document volume" tells a buyer exactly where the product fits. "AI-powered legal software for better productivity" tells them nothing.

**Why a Locked Category Matters**

This matters even more once a vendor starts submitting to multiple directories at once. Without a locked primary category, the same product can end up labeled legal research on one profile, contract management on another, and generic legal AI on a third — and now the brand looks confused rather than confident.
