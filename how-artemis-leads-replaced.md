  <h1>How a B2B Agency Fixed Its Invisible Backlink Profile</h1>

  <img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/eac1ed8195a70c82ef1cb1be4047bbdf.png" alt="How a B2B Agency Fixed Its Invisible Backlink Profile">

  <p>Most B2B agencies pour their energy into client work and quietly ignore their own visibility. That was the case for Artemis Leads, a Barcelona-based outbound lead generation agency, until its founder realized that a strong website with real testimonials wasn't enough — the brand simply wasn't showing up anywhere else.</p>

  <p>The fix wasn't a random blast of backlinks. Instead, the agency worked through a gradual, reviewed rollout of directory placements across agency marketplaces, startup databases, and business profile sites. Each addition was chosen deliberately rather than dumped in bulk, which let the team keep control over where their name appeared.</p>

  <p>The result, according to the founder's own account, was a jump in Ahrefs Domain Rating from roughly 1 to 34 in under a year — alongside a noticeable rise in inbound leads, some of which reportedly came through AI-powered discovery tools. This kind of outcome is documented in a detailed <a href="https://listingbott.com/blog/artemis-leads-listingbott-case-study/">case study on directory-driven growth</a>, which walks through exactly which platforms mattered and why.</p>

  <p>The takeaway for other agencies: a great offer only converts if people — and increasingly, AI systems — can find consistent proof of it across the web.</p>
