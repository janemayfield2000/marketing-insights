<h2>Why SEO Has Always Been Hard for Busy Founders</h2>

<p>SEO isn't one task — it's a dozen ongoing tasks stacked on top of each other, each requiring different expertise: keyword research your audience actually searches, content produced regularly enough that search engines notice, internal links that connect your pages usefully, and backlinks from other sites, which typically means outreach or paid placements. All of it needs to happen consistently, for months, before results show up.</p>

<p>For a solo founder or small team, that workload is usually impossible to sustain. Content gets written in bursts and abandoned. Keyword research gets skipped. Internal linking gets forgotten. The result is a blog that technically exists but never moves the needle on organic traffic.</p>
