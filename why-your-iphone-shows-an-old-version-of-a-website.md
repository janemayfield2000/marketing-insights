<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #111; margin-bottom: 16px;">Why Your iPhone Shows an Old Version of a Website (And How to Fix It in 30 Seconds)</h1>

<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/411f1f0863d5d6ed3b33a1ae8ffb867a.png" alt="iPhone Website Cache" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">If you've ever refreshed a page on your iPhone and still seen yesterday's layout, you're not imagining things. Browsers store local copies of pages — images, scripts, and style files — so sites load faster on repeat visits. The problem is that this same shortcut can backfire the moment a site gets updated, leaving you staring at a version that no longer exists on the server.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">The fastest way to check whether this is happening is to open the page in private browsing mode. If the content looks different there than in your normal tab, the culprit is local data, not the website itself. This single test saves a lot of guesswork before you start blaming the site owner or your Wi-Fi.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Once you've confirmed it's a local issue, the fix is simple: clear the stored data for that one site instead of wiping your whole browser. This keeps your other logins and saved preferences intact while resolving the specific glitch. For a full breakdown of how to do this in Safari, Chrome, and Firefox, this <a href="https://unicornplatform.com/blog/how-to-clear-website-data-on-iphone/" style="color: #4f46e5; text-decoration: none; font-weight: 600;">step-by-step guide to clearing website data on iPhone</a> walks through each browser's exact settings path.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">It's worth remembering that this is a diagnostic step, not a permanent fix. If a page keeps reverting to old content after every clear, the real issue is likely on the publishing side — a caching layer, a CDN delay, or a mismatched page slug. Clearing data just tells you where to look next.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">For anyone who edits or publishes web pages regularly, building this check into your routine pays off quickly. A ten-second private-mode test can save you an entire afternoon of troubleshooting the wrong problem.</p>
